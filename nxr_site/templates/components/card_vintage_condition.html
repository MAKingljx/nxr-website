NXR Vintage Card Classifications

Four archival classifications for vintage cards. Age-related patina is regarded as authentic character, not damage.

I
Pristine

Near-pristine vintage survivor. Almost no age-related patina. Sharp corners, crisp edges and clean surface. Free of creases, scuffs, stains and paper loss.

II
Nova

Displays natural age patina matching its era. Minor soft corner rounding and trivial edge wear. No creases or tears. Card surface stays fully intact.

III
Legacy

Noticeable corner rounding and edge wear with moderate age patina. Faint soft indentations may appear. No hard creases and no paper loss.

IV
Helix

Carries honest traces of past collector enjoyment. Heavy corner and edge wear, together with rich age patina. Visible creases, surface scuffs and minor spots are acceptable. The card remains structurally complete, without major tears or missing pieces.
